About HN

HNwas founded in 2007. The gracefully formal restaurant caters to the varied tastes of the international traveler. Serving global cuisine, it offers the choicest of Indian, Chinese, Continental and Mediterranean delicacies 🥘🥘🥘.

Multi-cuisine

Where was the first multi-cusine restaurant in Kollam?

​The first multi-cuisine restaurant in Kollam was Allspice, established in 2002 by Supreme Food Co. This restaurant introduced a diverse range of global flavors to the city, marking a significant milestone in Kollam's culinary history.
